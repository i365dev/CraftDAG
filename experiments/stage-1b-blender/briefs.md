# Fixed Stage 1A benchmark briefs

All briefs have the same level of detail and target a 64-block vertical extent. Each asks for one readable, useful silhouette and names only the major parts.

1. **Simple house** — Create a compact two-storey timber house with a rectangular footprint, visible entrance, windows, and a pitched roof. Keep the roof, walls, and openings easy to distinguish from outside.
2. **Castle tower** — Create a single medieval stone watchtower with a broad base, tall shaft, an upper lookout, battlements, and a narrow entrance. Keep the vertical tower silhouette clear.
3. **Ship / vehicle silhouette** — Create a small wooden sailing ship with a tapered hull, deck, one mast, and two sails. Make the hull and mast readable from the side and three-quarter views.
4. **Statue / humanoid monument** — Create a monumental standing humanoid statue on a simple plinth, with a distinct head, torso, two arms, and two legs. Keep the pose and body parts readable at a distance.
5. **Dragon / organic creature** — Create a perched fantasy dragon facing sideways, with a distinct head and neck, body, four legs, long tail, and two large wings. Favor a strong outer silhouette over tiny scales or spikes.
